- [The malleability of spatial skills: a meta-analysis of training studies (Uttal et al.)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22663761/). The basis of the Mental rotation exercise
- [Working memory training revisited: A multi-level meta-analysis of n-back training studies (Soveri et al.)](https://doi.org/10.3758/s13423-016-1217-0). Psychonomic Bulletin & Review · 2017 — 33 randomized controlled trials: n-back training transfers moderately to untrained n-back tasks and very little to other memory and reasoning tasks; much of the gain is task-specific. The basis and the limit of the Memory stream exercise
