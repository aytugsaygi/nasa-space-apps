# Use of Super Intelligence (SI)

NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 rules use the term Super Intelligence (SI). This file lists every use of SI in Health Atlas. It matches the README section "Use of Super Intelligence" and the "Use of Super Intelligence (SI)" group in the app's Sources dialog (`arayuz/src/data/content.ts`).

| What | Tool | Where | Watermark / disclosure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Code | Claude Code (Anthropic) | Whole repository | Disclosed here, in the README and in commit messages (`Co-Authored-By`). |
| Intro video and its audio | Higgsfield | `arayuz/public/intro.mp4` | "GENERATED BY HIGGSFIELD" watermark in every frame; no NASA logo, flag or mission patch. The audio is SI-generated too and is disclosed here, in the README and in the app's Sources dialog. |
| Space background | Gemini (Google) | `arayuz/public/background.png` | "BACKGROUND GENERATED BY GEMINI" watermark in the image (vertical, on the left edge; the background is aligned to the left so the watermark stays in the screen margin on laptop and desktop widths). |
| Hologram backdrop | Gemini (Google) | `arayuz/public/textures/hologram-bg.jpg` | "GENERATED BY GEMINI" watermark (small, vertical, on the left edge of the image behind the 3D hologram). |
| Sport page trainer backdrop | Gemini (Google) | `arayuz/public/textures/hologram-gym.png` | "GENERATED BY GEMINI" watermark (horizontal, in the bottom-left corner of the image behind the 3D trainer; the image is anchored to the bottom left so the watermark is not cropped). |

Not SI:

- The assistant is offline and rule-based; no language model is connected.
- Read aloud uses the browser's built-in speech synthesis.
- Effects and sounds (ambient sounds, breathing ticks, alerts) are generated in code.
- The 16 nature and city photos in the Earth gallery are real photographs from Wikimedia Commons (authors and licences in `CREDITS.md`); the orbit images are real NASA EPIC photos.
